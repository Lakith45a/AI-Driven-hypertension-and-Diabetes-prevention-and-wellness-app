Hypertension Staging Assessment Portal

Lifestyle Questionnaire

Answer six quick questions covering stress, salt intake and coping behaviours. The questionnaire will compute a stress score and a salt-intake score (0 = low, 10 = high) and prefill the hypertension form.

  1. 1. How often do you feel overwhelmed by tasks?
  2. 2. How well do you sleep most nights?
  3. 3. How often do you feel anxious or worried?
  4. 4. How often do you use unhealthy coping (excessive alcohol, tobacco, overeating)?
  5. 5. How supported do you feel by friends/family?
  6. 6. How much salt do you typically add/use in your diet?
